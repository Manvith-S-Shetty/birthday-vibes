# Reference Analysis — 2Luv Birthday Flow

## Source

https://www.2-luv.com/en/create/birthday

## What we should learn from it

### 1. Simple start
Do not overwhelm the creator with 20 fields immediately.

### 2. Personalization is the product
Name, photos, music and message should drive the experience.

### 3. Preview before publish
The creator should see what the recipient will receive.

### 4. Private sharing
A birthday surprise benefits from a protected experience.

### 5. Interactive moments
The experience should contain things the recipient can discover and interact with, not just read.

### 6. Shareability
A unique link and QR code make the experience easy to send.

## What we should NOT copy

Do not reproduce:
- 2Luv branding
- exact page layout
- exact copy
- exact visual assets
- exact animations
- proprietary implementation
- identical theme names or compositions

We use the product category and UX principles as inspiration, then create an original visual language.

## Competitive insight

Other current birthday experience products emphasize PIN-protected surprises, photo galleries, music, interactive cake/candle moments, letters, shareable links and QR codes. These patterns validate the direction, but our differentiation should come from art direction and experience quality rather than feature count. citeturn0search4turn0search5

## Product differentiation

Our strongest differentiator:

> **A birthday website that feels like a short cinematic film made for one person.**

Not:
> "Choose a template and fill a form."
